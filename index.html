Food-Tech Product

OrderIn

A whole restaurant run from one screen — and customers who order simply by scanning a QR code.

OrderIn is a comprehensive, end-to-end restaurant management platform built around two connected experiences — a powerful owner's dashboard and an effortless QR-based customer ordering flow, with no app to download. Here is every service it provides, explained in full.

Everything OrderIn does

The services, in detail

QR-Code Ordering

Customers scan a QR code at the table or counter — no app, no sign-up — and browse a rich menu with images, categories and special-instruction notes. Accounts are tied simply to a phone number, so returning guests instantly see their history and saved favourites.

Dynamic Menu Management

A living digital menu you update anytime from any device — add dishes with image, description, category, veg/non-veg type and price. An availability toggle hides sold-out items without deleting them, an all-veg toggle flips the whole menu vegetarian, and the module is locked behind its own sub-login.

Illusion Pricing

A single toggle shows the real price beside a higher, struck-through 'original' price — signalling a great deal to customers without you actually lowering your earnings. It's the proven psychological pricing used by big hospitality brands, now one tap away.

In-App Advertising

Create full promotional banners that pop up on the customer's ordering page — today's special, a festival offer, a seasonal deal — with a custom run-duration. It puts in-house marketing in your hands with no external tools or agency needed.

Order Management & Live Status

Every order — placed by a customer's phone or entered manually by staff — flows through one pipeline: Pending → Preparing → Ready → Delivered, kept in sync across kitchen, floor and the customer's own screen. Manual order entry covers walk-ups and phone-less guests with zero double-entry.

Inventory & Flash Checks

Log every ingredient with live quantities and get Low-Stock and Very-Low-Stock alerts before you run out mid-service. See current levels at a glance, auto-clear items at zero, and keep it all accountable behind a dedicated inventory sub-login.

Flexible Payments

Three ways to pay so you never lose a sale — secure online payment via Razorpay, physical card at your terminal, or cash. Every method is verified, and it's built for both Indian and European markets.

Counter-Code Verification

For cash and card, a unique code is generated at the counter and entered by the customer to confirm real payment before the order hits the kitchen. This closes the 'I already paid' loophole and protects your revenue during the busiest rushes.

Bill Generation

After payment, customers get a clean digital bill they can download, save or share — cutting paper and improving record-keeping. Every order is stored, so any past bill can be reviewed or reprinted at any time.

Finance, Earnings & Settlements

A Daily Transit view gives a live 24-hour financial heartbeat, with earnings broken down by online, card and cash across any date range. The Razorpay ledger shows each pending settlement and its estimated arrival date — so you can plan cash flow without chasing your provider. The platform fee is charged to the customer, so your menu earnings stay fully yours.

Feedback & Google Ratings

Non-intrusive feedback prompts route straight into your dashboard's notifications, so no comment or concern slips by. Positive reviews also feed your Google rating — quietly building the online reputation that drives new customers.

Role-Based Access & Security

A primary login plus dedicated sub-logins on Menu, Inventory and Finance mean each staff member only touches what they should — a waiter sees orders but can't change prices, only a manager opens earnings. It protects you from both internal errors and outside misuse.

Who it serves: the food & restaurant industry worldwide — reaching both owners and diners, from independent cafés and small chains to food trucks, cloud kitchens, and self-service outlets.

Our team handles the complete onboarding — menu setup, dashboard configuration, and training — before you go live.

Edu-Tech Product

Ansirk

One smart app that runs an entire school — and makes learning engaging, personal, and in the child's own language.

Ansirk is an India-first school ERP and learning platform delivered as a single dynamic app that adapts to each school. It spans five connected dashboards — Admin, Management, Teachers, Parents and Students — handling the whole running of a school while turning learning into an engaging, game-like experience. Here is every service it provides, explained in full.

Everything Ansirk does

The services, in detail

One App, Five Dashboards

A single dynamic application serves Admin, School Management, Teachers, Parents and Students — no separate apps. After login it rebrands to each school automatically, loading that school's logo, theme and content from the backend.

School & Student Management

Add students manually or bulk-import by CSV/Excel, auto-generate their logins, and run the year-end promotion workflow with controlled exceptions and pass-outs. Dropouts and rejoins are tracked with full history, and health records live alongside each profile.

Teacher Management & Roles

Add teachers with qualifications and assign roles — Principal, Year In-charge, Class Teacher, Subject Teacher — that precisely scope what each can see and do. Build timetables, approve or reject leave, and assign a substitute the moment leave is granted.

Fees & Finance

Track paid, pending and upcoming fees per student across tuition, canteen, hostel and event types, with online payment auto-syncing from parents via Razorpay. Create new fee types on the fly with auto-notices, generate and reprint bills, and auto-remind parents 7, 3 and 1 days before a due date.

Digital Diary & Circulars

Replaces the paper school diary — teachers post daily notes and circulars that appear instantly in parent and student dashboards. Parents acknowledge with a single tap, and management can see exactly who has read what.

Notifications & Communication

Send targeted messages to individuals, classes, groups, teachers or parents with multi-filter precision, and receive replies right inside the thread. An automatic morning digest keeps everyone aligned each day.

Live Bus Tracking & Field-Trip Mode

Map bus routes on Google Maps, link driver numbers, and let parents and students watch the bus move in real time via the driver's GPS. Field-Trip Mode sets a start and end point, navigates the driver, and lets everyone monitor whether the route is being followed.

Face-Recognition Attendance

Teachers mark their own attendance through Firebase ML Kit face recognition, with a manual fallback whenever needed. Student attendance is recorded per class and visible day-by-day to parents.

Timetable, Calendar & Results

Build per-section timetables and a full academic calendar of exams, events and holidays — including emergency closures — pushed live to every dashboard. Results are entered per class with subject-wise analytics feeding each student's growth picture.

Practice Zone — Gamified Learning

Textbook content becomes topic-wise MCQ levels that unlock progressively, with a redo if a student gets three wrong in a row and a 'Boss Level' every six to seven stages. Streaks and stage-style progression make revision feel like a game — and poems stay in their original language with word-for-word meaning.

AI Doubt Center

Powered by Gemini with access to the school's own uploaded curriculum, students can ask a doubt by typing, speaking, or photographing a handwritten question (read by Gemini Vision). Answers come back grade-appropriate and in any Indian language — English and regional shown together when helpful.

Career Guidance AI

For students in the final grade, Gemini analyses their full academic history — practice and test patterns, strengths and interests — to produce a Career Suitability Report. It deliberately surfaces diverse paths based on real ability, pushing back against the engineer/doctor tunnel-vision.

India-First & Multilingual

Built for India first — 10+ languages including Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam, Kannada, Marathi and Sanskrit, with English and the regional language shown together. It supports CBSE, State and ICSE boards and local payment methods out of the box.

Growth & Well-Being First

Ansirk rewards improvement, not just toppers — there are no public leaderboards or academic comparisons, protecting each child's mental health. Students who show big growth get recognition, and class-monitor rotation builds leadership instead of ranking.

Who it serves: school education in India, pre-primary to Grade 10 — reaching schools, teachers, parents and students, and brought in by owners, principals and administrators. It serves schools only, not colleges.
